## Reviewer summaries

**test quality, determinism, coverage** (25 findings, 53 files read in full). The suite is large and in many areas carefully built: the scripted HTTP/2 adversarial server, the TLS negative PKI with positive controls, the skip gate with a per-target baseline, AssertAsync bounds and the fuzz, soak, Autobahn and interop harnesses are solid, and there are no [Ignore]/[Explicit] attributes at all (every skip is a run-time [env] precondition).
